FAQ

Common questions

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Every client gets a fixed monthly quote after a free review, because books that look simple are not always simple. As a guide, simple monthly books for a small business start from $299/month.

Do I need to switch accounting software?

No. RSBS works in QuickBooks Online, Sage 50, Sage Intacct, Yardi, Zoho Books and SAP. QuickBooks Online is recommended for new clients.

Can you fix books that are months behind?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup is quoted as a one-time project, then you can move to a monthly plan.

Do you meet in person?

Yes, in Ottawa, Nepean and Barrhaven, or fully remote if you prefer.
